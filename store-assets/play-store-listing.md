## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Aliasist Files Abductor is a straightforward downloader for YouTube videos
and direct file URLs — no clutter, no fake "download" buttons that lead
nowhere, no bundled junk.

🛸 HOW IT WORKS
Paste a URL, tap Abduct File, watch the progress bar. When it's done, the
app hands the file to your device's share sheet so you can save it wherever
you want — Downloads, Google Drive, wherever.

📼 SUPPORTS
• YouTube videos (best available quality, merged video+audio)
• Direct file URLs

⚠️ YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
You're responsible for only downloading content you have the right to
download. This app is a tool, not a license to pirate — see the in-app
disclaimer and our privacy policy.

Built by Aliasist. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
